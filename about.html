About Myanmar 2D 3D

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Myanmar 2D 3D, a premier informational and statistical utility designed to deliver lightning-fast, transparent, and accurate market data synchronization for international users and enthusiasts.

Play Store Policy & Compliance Declaration

Myanmar 2D 3D is strictly an informational, educational, and statistical tracking application. We do NOT facilitate, operate, manage, promote, or encourage real-money gambling, lottery betting, or financial transactions of any kind. No wagers, deposits, or payouts can be made within the application. All statistical figures, charts, and historical numbers presented are intended solely for personal analysis and entertainment.

Our Mission

Our goal is to leverage modern mobile technology to offer real-time data transparency, instant synchronization, and a clean, responsive interface. Designed with performance and user experience in mind, our system ensures you stay up-to-date with publicly accessible financial indexes and historical records without lag or latency.

Core Capabilities & Services

Myanmar 2D 3D integrates multiple essential utility modules into a single, intuitive platform:

  • Real-Time 2D Live Data: Direct real-time index mirroring public Stock Exchange of Thailand (SET) morning (12:01 PM) and afternoon (4:30 PM) market sessions.
  • 2D Historical Archive: Comprehensive daily logs including SET values, index movements, Modern, and Internet numerical combinations.
  • 3D Historical Calendar (1971 - 2026): An extensive multi-decade historical archive structured by years and draw dates for longitudinal statistical review.
  • Thai Government Lottery Checker: An easy-to-use 6-digit ticket verification tool for tracking official government lottery draws.
  • Dream Book (Symbolic Dictionary): A traditional cultural dream-symbolism reference index associating visual symbols with numerical counterparts.
  • Interactive Community Room: A real-time moderated chat environment enabling users to share perspectives, insights, and analytical discussions.

Data Source Transparency & Attribution

All stock index calculations, trade values, and opening/closing figures displayed in this application are derived directly from the public data feeds of the Stock Exchange of Thailand (SET - settrade.com). Myanmar 2D 3D is an independent developer entity and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Stock Exchange of Thailand (SET) or the Government Lottery Office (GLO) of Thailand.
